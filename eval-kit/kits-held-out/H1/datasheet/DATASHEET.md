# Datasheet for Paperwren (v1.1)

> Synthetic test material. All people, institutions, venues, datasets and results are fictional.

## Composition

Paperwren v1.1 contains **12,384 forms** in 12 layout families. Each form has between 6 and 19 fields (mean 11.4).

| Split | Forms |
| --- | ---: |
| train | 8,640 |
| val | 1,200 |
| test | 2,544 |
| total | 12,384 |

Per-family counts are in `layout_families.csv`. Split counts are in `splits.csv`.

## Collection

The form layouts and the values to write were generated by script. 37 paid annotators in 3 countries filled in the printed forms by hand; the forms were then scanned and labelled field by field, and every label was checked by a second annotator.

## Uses

Intended: training and evaluating form-reading models. Not allowed: identifying annotators from their handwriting, or any use outside research (see `../access/ACCESS.md`).

## Limitations

- **English only.** Every form is in English; scores do not transfer to other languages or scripts.
- **Synthetic layouts.** The twelve layout families are designed, not collected; real forms are messier.
- **Small annotator pool.** 37 annotators from 3 countries wrote all forms, so handwriting styles are limited.
