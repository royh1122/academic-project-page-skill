# Getting access to Paperwren

Paperwren is **not** an open download. The handwriting belongs to our annotators, and the data-use agreement forbids trying to identify anyone from it.

1. Fill in the request form: https://paperwren.example.org/request-access
2. Sign the data-use agreement that we e-mail back to you (one per research group).
3. We review requests by hand. Approval takes about 5 working days.
4. Once approved, you receive a personal download token. Tokens expire after 90 days; ask for a new one if yours runs out.

The forms and labels for train and validation are included. Test labels are withheld for the leaderboard (see `data/README_DATA.md`).
