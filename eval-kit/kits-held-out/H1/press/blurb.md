# Paperwren: press blurb (draft for the department newsletter)

Researchers from four institutions have released Paperwren, a new benchmark of 12,384 handwritten forms that tests how well AI systems read messy, real-looking paperwork. Paperwren is publicly available, and the team hopes it will become the standard test for form reading.

"Forms are everywhere, and they are still hard for machines," says Oskar Esimerkki.
